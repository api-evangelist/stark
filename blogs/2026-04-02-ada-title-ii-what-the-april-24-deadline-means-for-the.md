On April 24, 2026, the DOJ's ADA Title II rule goes into effect. For state and local governments serving populations over 50,000, WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance is no longer purely advisory — it's federal law. For every software team building, supporting, or selling into those public sector organizations, that obligation is closer to home than most currently recognize.
